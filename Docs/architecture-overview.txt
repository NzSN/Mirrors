Compiler, generated evaluator binding, client codecs, suite and configuration
are trusted. Gate loads the submitted adapter inside the restricted worker.
The evaluator uses distinct model, Gate-control and public-port RPC protocols.
Gate retains the original owner until physical cleanup; permission profiles are
not permanent machines. The model service may run on a different trusted host.

ACCEPTANCE AND OPERATING LIMITS
Reported-state matches do not prove observation fidelity. Protect the observer
closure, probe real SUT facts independently, and require rejection of real SUT
mutants and a shadow-observer control. Retain exact finite denominators.
Normal cleanup is separate from offline filesystem and post-crash process
recovery. Ambiguous owner loss never permits reconstruction of a pass.
Per-process/UID limits and live-job capacity do not provide a whole-system
memory, disk or retained-result budget. The optional service has finite epoch
bounds; direct callers require their own admission and retention owner.
Persistent per-author/corpus attempt budgets use synchronized spent markers;
epoch-local start/rate limits alone do not survive restart.
The job store separately reserves 256 outcomes of at most 262144 encoded bytes
each (64 MiB), preserving the separate 65535-byte wire delivery limit.
V2 process claims bind an exact cgroup; only durable observed-empty cleanup
reclaims linked processes. Legacy/foreign/unpublished claims stay ambiguous.
Shared unattended or cross-restart adaptive evaluation requires separately
accepted aggregate, retention, process-recovery and disclosure-budget mechanisms.
See Docs/mirrors-gate-boundary.md for profile admission and authoritative sources.
